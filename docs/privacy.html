AIPChart Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026

AIPChart is a hobbyist app that shows aviation charts with your position on them. It is developed by Daniyaal Khan ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your location and your charts stay on your device, the app has no accounts, analytics or ads, and I don't collect personal data.

Location

  • The app asks for your device's location so it can show where you are on a chart or the en-route map, and which charts cover your position.
  • Location is used only while the app is open. The app does not request background location.
  • Your location is processed on your device only. It is never sent to me or to anyone else, and it is not stored.
  • You can refuse or revoke the permission at any time in your device's settings. The app still works for viewing charts.

Charts and files on your device

Airports you add, charts you download or import, and the reference points you place on them are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Files you import with the file picker are copied into that storage and never uploaded. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of it.

Chart downloads

When you browse or download charts, the app requests chart lists and PDF files from charts.daniyaalkhan.com. These are plain downloads of public files: they contain no account, device identifier or location. As with any website, the server and its hosting provider, Cloudflare, receive technical information needed to deliver the files, such as your IP address, the time of the request and the file requested. Cloudflare may keep this in its logs for security purposes, such as blocking abuse; see Cloudflare's privacy policy. I don't use this information to identify you and don't combine it with anything else.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting services.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with anyone.

Email and links

If you tap the email address or the GitHub links in the app, they open in your own email app or browser. If you email me, I'll use your message and address only to reply and to consider your suggestion. Contributions on GitHub are subject to GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

AIPChart is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
